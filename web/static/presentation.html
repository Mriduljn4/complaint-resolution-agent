Autonomous Banking Complaint Resolution Copilot

The Enterprise Challenge & Compliance Stakes

  • High Dispute Volumes: US retail banks process hundreds of thousands of disputes across Checking/Savings, Credit Cards, and P2P Transfers annually.
  • Strict Statutory Regimes: Federal law imposes mandatory investigation deadlines under 12 CFR § 1005.11 (Reg E) and 12 CFR § 1026.13 (Reg Z).
  • Cost of Failure: Missed statutory deadlines trigger severe CFPB enforcement actions, statutory civil damages, and mandatory consumer restitution.

The Solution & Core Architectural Principle

  • Probabilistic Text Interpretation: Azure OpenAI gpt-4.1 & Azure AI Foundry parse unstructured consumer narratives against official CFPB taxonomy.
  • Deterministic Compliance Math: Deadlines and calendar math are computed strictly via pure code (Azure Functions / MCP Tool) to eliminate hallucinations.
  • Granular Grounding: Every draft resolution cites the exact eCFR regulatory paragraph chunk retrieved from Azure AI Search.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Governance: High-risk cases are gated by mandatory Azure Bot Service Adaptive Card sign-offs.

Slide 3 of 8 • Agentic Design & Risk Matrix

Two-Agent Orchestration & Policy-Driven Risk Matrix

Why Two Separate Agents in Foundry?

  • Cognitive Separation: Isolating intake classification from legal drafting prevents prompt bloat and eliminates confirmation bias.
  • Triage Agent: Classifies complaint narrative into CFPB Product & Issue values and evaluates risk parameters against config/risk_rules.yaml.
  • Resolution Agent: Queries paragraph knowledge base, calls deadline tool, and formats empathetic customer reply citing statutory paragraph.
  • Fallback Guardrail: Discretionary/subjective disputes return "Needs specialist review" rather than hallucinating regulatory citations.

Config-Driven Risk Policy (config/risk_rules.yaml)

  • High Risk Triggers
    • Disputed transaction amount $\ge \$1,000.00$
    • Suspected identity theft or fraudulent account opening
    • Debanking, complete account freeze, or deprivation of living funds
    • Wire transfer scams, unauthorized mobile wallet takeovers
    • Protected cohorts: Older Americans or Servicemembers
  • Normal Risk Routine fee inquiries, minor debit/ATM disputes, standard purchase disputes auto-clear.
Slide 4 of 8 • Regulatory Grounding (Part A)

Regulatory Grounding: Reg E vs. Reg Z & Paragraph Indexing

Domain Lookup Table (Specification Mandate)

  • Credit card $\longrightarrow$ Regulation Z (12 CFR § 1026.13)
    Truth in Lending Act / Fair Credit Billing Act
  • Checking or savings account $\longrightarrow$ Regulation E (12 CFR § 1005.11)
    Electronic Fund Transfer Act (EFT)
  • Money transfer / Cash App $\longrightarrow$ Regulation E (12 CFR § 1005.11)
  • Zero Cross-Pollination: Prevents credit disputes from citing debit rules and debit disputes from citing credit billing cycles.

Paragraph-Level Chunks in Azure AI Search

  • 1005.11(a): Definition of EFT Error
  • 1005.11(b): 60-day consumer error notice window
  • 1005.11(c)(1): Standard 10-business-day investigation deadline
  • 1005.11(c)(2): 45/90 calendar day provisional credit extension
  • 1005.11(c)(3): 20-business-day new account extension
  • 1026.13(c)(1): 30 calendar day credit acknowledgment
  • 1026.13(c)(2): 2 billing cycles resolution window (max 90 days)
  • Done When Verified "How long does bank have to investigate account error?" returns 1005.11(c)(1) with eCFR URL.
Slide 5 of 8 • Deadline Tool (Part C)

Deterministic Statutory Deadline Engine (Part C)

Why Deadlines Must Never Be Left to an LLM

  • The Core Vulnerability: LLMs are probabilistic token predictors that hallucinate date arithmetic, confuse calendar vs business days, and fail to calculate holiday observances.
  • Statutory Arithmetic: Compliance deadlines are legal mandates with severe civil penalties for one-day errors.
  • Exposed Interfaces:
    • Local Python engine (src/deadline_tool.py)
    • Azure Function / REST API (/api/calculate-deadline)
    • MCP Tool (Model Context Protocol) for dynamic agent invocation.

Calendar Intelligence & Explainable Working

  • Weekend Exclusion: Saturday & Sunday excluded for business-day calculations.
  • OPM US Federal Holidays (5 U.S.C. 6103): Fully observes New Year's, MLK Day, Washington's Bday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, July 4th, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas.
  • Weekend Roll Rules: Saturday holidays observed on Friday; Sunday holidays observed on Monday.
  • Full Explainability: Output explicitly provides start date, business days counted, weekend days skipped, and federal holidays skipped.
Slide 6 of 8 • Human-in-the-Loop & Traceability

Human-in-the-Loop Governance & End-to-End Traceability

Azure Bot Service & Adaptive Card Workflow

  • Gated Workflow: High-risk cases are paused in Pending status and cannot be delivered to customers without compliance approval.
  • Adaptive Card Dispatch: Dispatched via Azure Bot Service (complaint-resolution-agent) directly into Microsoft Teams.
  • Reviewer Controls: Compliance officers review dispute details, cited regulation, and draft reply, clicking [Approve] or [Reject with Comments].
  • Normal-Risk Pass-Through: Normal risk cases auto-approve immediately, preventing reviewer fatigue.

Application Insights Spans & Section 5 Audit Trail

  • OpenTelemetry Spans: Every stage generates an observable execution span with latencies exported to Application Insights:
    input_guardrail → triage_agent → regulatory_search → deadline_tool → reply_generation → approval_flow
  • Section 5 Immutable Audit Record: Logged to data/audit_log.jsonl with:
    • Complaint ID
    • Rule Cited & eCFR URL
    • Due Date & Working
    • Model (gpt-4.1) & Prompt Version (v1.2.0)
    • Decision, Approver & Timestamp UTC.
